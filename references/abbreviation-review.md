# Abbreviation and first-use consistency audit

## Establish scope and a source of truth

Treat the abstract, main text and each supplementary document as independent
reading units unless the user or publication specifies another convention.
A definition in the abstract does not carry into the body. A figure or table
should be interpretable from its labels and accompanying caption, legend or
note. Check titles and headings as well as prose. If a title would become
unwieldy, write it out or use neutral wording there and introduce the short
form at its first useful occurrence in the text.

Build a ledger of abbreviation, verified expansion, concept, exact case,
source of truth, first location in each unit, and locations in displays.
Prefer the manuscript's designated glossary, terminology table or documented
project convention for study labels. Cross-check ambiguity against the method,
display and definitions. For external organisations or standards, use their
official name. Record differences in scope or qualifiers, and do not infer a
full name from its initials. Mark unresolved scientific decisions **待人工确认** and identify the
evidence still needed.

## Check first use and recurrence

- Introduce needed technical shorthand as `full name (ABBR)` at first use in
  each unit. Check the singular/plural form, hyphenation, exact case and
  immediate referent. A glossary entry counts where the glossary is an
  explicit and accessible key, not as a retroactive definition of earlier
  prose.
- Find all occurrences of a form and both abbreviated and spelled-out names.
  Check for two concepts sharing one form, inconsistent codes for one concept,
  swapped categories, and expansions that drop qualifiers. Check mixed-case
  labels, digit-bearing terms, plurals and hyphenated compounds.
- Keep valid local definitions. In continuous prose, subsequent expansion is
  usually unnecessary. Use the full name alone if a new acronym would be used
  only once. Repeating a definition in another independent unit can be useful.
- Classify symbols, units, panel labels, standard codes, citation abbreviations,
  model and product names, and common shorthand separately. Preserve formal
  identifiers, bibliographic names, quotations and executable search strings.
  A metric symbol can need an operational gloss rather than an invented
  expansion. Follow journal and audience conventions.

## Inspect display items and Word reading order

Inventory captions, legends, panel and axis labels, table headers and notes,
footnotes, text boxes, vector artwork and images. Link each item to the
correct figure or table. Choose the shortest explanation that lets the intended
reader identify the labels and interpret the display. Consider whether the
caption remains easy to scan after adding definitions.

- For standardised country codes, identify the coding convention once, for
  example by naming the standard. For widely recognised technical symbols,
  state the applicable convention, unit or scale where needed. Individual
  expansions are unnecessary when that context is sufficient. Explain only
  exceptions, ambiguous forms or project-specific codes.
- For study-specific labels, give a compact verified key in the caption or
  legend if the figure must be interpreted independently. Keep this material
  near the display. Supplementary figures and tables may share an explicitly
  identified key within the supplement when the requested use allows it.
- Compare the exact forms in the artwork, caption and relevant text. Flag
  mismatches with the figure, panel and exact labels. Show the authoritative
  definition or explain what remains uncertain. Mark changes to scientific
  terminology or label-to-concept mappings **待人工确认**, and obtain
  confirmation before a separate revision task applies them. Adding a verified
  expansion that preserves the existing term and meaning is an editorial change.

For DOCX, reconstruct the reading order of Word text runs and paragraphs,
including tables and text boxes. Treat inline fields, equations and tracked
revisions separately so extraction neither splits a label into fragments nor
joins unrelated strings. Inspect embedded raster or vector labels visually or
with reliable OCR and verify mixed-case or ambiguous characters against the
image. Figure alt text and caption text alone do not establish what is visible
in the artwork. Record inaccessible or illegible items as **无法检查**.

## Report findings within the authorised scope

For each confirmed error give the reading unit, exact location and original,
the evidence for the intended term, and the direction of correction. Explain
where a definition belongs and what a compact key should cover. Identify
optional glosses separately. A missing definition required for independent
reading, a wrong expansion, or misleading competing meanings warrants a
finding. Common shorthand alone does not. Check the likely impact on nearby
first uses, existing display keys and protected scientific terms. Use the
[report contract](report-contract.md) and keep all submissions read-only.
