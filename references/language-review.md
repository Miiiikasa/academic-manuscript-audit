# Natural English and a consistent authorial voice

## Diagnose before recommending

For a candidate sentence identify its claim, paragraph job and protected
invariants. Name a real defect and identify the smallest affected unit. Evaluate
potential changes internally for scientific equivalence and paragraph fit. Give
the repair direction in the report. Clear and natural prose passes the language check. For
necessity, placement and argument hierarchy, apply the separate
[whole-paper tests](whole-paper-review.md).

Reportable defects are grammar errors; clearly unnatural phrasing; ambiguity
that alters or blocks understanding; empty repetition; inconsistent terminology
for the same measured object; and a demonstrable mismatch between wording and
evidence. Word counts and phrase hits do not establish these defects.

## Contextual signals, never a blacklist

| Signal | Inspect for | Preserve when |
|---|---|---|
| “These findings highlight…”, “It is important to note…”, “plays a crucial role…” | Empty emphasis, hidden comparison or inflated consequence | The sentence states a supported consequence or boundary in concrete terms |
| “Together”, “further”, “therefore”, “nevertheless” | Restatement or unsupported relation | Arithmetic addition, genuine synthesis, transition or inference |
| “not only… but also…”, “not merely…”, “rather than…” | Decorative negation or manufactured opposition | A real contrast, exclusion, denominator distinction or measurement limit |
| Three-part lists and symmetric clauses | Artificial cadence or duplicate concepts | Items are distinct analytical dimensions or necessary definitions |
| Abstract noun chains and nominalisations | Hidden action, unclear subject or added layer without information | A precise technical definition or useful conceptual synthesis |
| “markedly”, “relatively”, “comparatively”, “important” | Missing comparator or unearned judgement | The comparison is clear from the sentence or immediate context |
| “In … research”, stacked possessives, literal prepositions | Translationese that impedes reading | The construction is idiomatic and the referent clear |
| Repeated openings or forced sentence variation | Mechanical rhythm or unnatural synonym cycling | Parallel methods or comparable results need parallel form |

For an empty frame, preserve the substantive statement, not a substitute frame.
Do not replace one vague summary with another. Prefer concrete subjects and verbs
where they clarify the action, but do not flatten all abstraction into lists of
facts. Generalisation is a legitimate job of Introduction and Discussion.

## Manuscript-wide style ledger

Use [abbreviation-review.md](abbreviation-review.md) for first-use scopes,
independent figure and table keys, and abbreviation-specific exceptions.

| Dimension | Record | Decision rule |
|---|---|---|
| Terminology | Concept, canonical name, definition, abbreviation, accepted short forms | Enforce stable meaning and define technical shorthand at first use in each reading unit |
| Quantities | Unit, annual/cumulative basis, denominator, counted object | Compare like with like; retain informative qualifiers |
| English variant | Applicable journal choice or the author's established consistent variant | Exempt proper names, titles, quotations and executable/search text |
| Tense | Procedures, observations, definitions, interpretation | Variation by function is normal; flag an unclear time reference |
| Claim strength | Design permits association, description, inference or causation | Keep uncertainty and attribution; no rhetorical strengthening |
| Register | Methods procedural, Results evidential, Discussion interpretive | Preserve section differences within one professional, restrained voice |
| Comparable information | Country names, interval labels, shares, metric introductions | Use parallel conventions when they help comparison, with natural syntax |

Defined short forms are legitimate. Determine whether two labels denote the
same object, a defined subset, or a useful contextual abbreviation. Check the
operational definition before recommending unification.

Apply the verified journal convention when specified. Otherwise respect the
established spelling and punctuation choices when internally consistent.
Without an explicit journal rule, explain any material mixture as a general
norm or optional P2 improvement. Do not impose a default spelling variant.
Preserve reference titles, dataset and model names, quotations and literal queries.

## Compare the writing across passages

Use paired original excerpts with locations. Start with similar rhetorical
tasks (section openings, equivalent comparisons, transitions or interpretation
sentences), then check the handoff between adjacent sections. Use several
passages when claiming a pattern. Describe the difference and locate the moment
it makes reading harder.

| Feature | Compare | Reader cost that can justify a finding |
|---|---|---|
| Sentence patterns | Grammatical subjects, leading clauses, order of result and qualification, list construction | Repeated restarts or shifting subject patterns obscure which claims are parallel |
| Information density | Distinct propositions, numbers, units and denominators per sentence or paragraph | The reader must infer the main contrast through several equally prominent details |
| Abstraction | Concrete comparison followed by interpretation, or generic labels in place of a relation | The reader has to recover the intended relation from a previous section |
| Stance | Authorial distance, modality and verbs of inference | Comparable evidence is presented with inconsistent certainty or unexplained changes of register |
| Transitions | Explicit logical relation, lexical link, generic framing or abrupt switch of task | The text announces a topic without revealing the next step in the argument |
| Rhythm | Buildup and release of detail, recurrence of sentence openings, where emphasis falls | Adjacent passages reintroduce context or bury the takeaway after long inventories |

Technical precision in Methods, denser evidence in Results and more abstract
interpretation in Discussion are expected. Diagnose a material mismatch only
where the functions are comparable or where a handoff obscures the argument.
Length and density are evidence to inspect, not thresholds. Preserve
appropriate variety and leave well-functioning patterns alone.

## Protect established editorial choices

Read the local decision/rejection ledger before suggesting edits. Do not repeat
rejected pure-style changes under a new rationale or rename them “consistency”.
A passed language check or prior preference decision does not settle placement or
necessity. Reopen only with a newly demonstrated wording defect or a specific
structural cost from the current comparison. State the evidence and preserve the
author's underlying decision when possible.
Do not equate an older reviewer's recommendation with an author's rejection.
When history is incomplete, disclose the gap and suppress preference-only edits.

Useful topic sentences and transitions, substantive synthesis, real contrasts,
numeric “together”, defined short forms and valid abstract summaries can
pass the language check. No random synonym changes, deliberate mistakes, forced sentence-length
variation, claims of AI authorship or AI-detector optimisation.

## Repair verification

Check values/precision, denominator, time window, population/group, unit,
citation, uncertainty, direction and term meaning. Then check paragraph purpose,
neighbouring sentences and later uses. If the repair changes an invariant, mark
**待人工确认** and state the scientific decision needed.
